\# 1\. Brand Philosophy

RED SEA DIGITAL is not a marketing agency.

It is a boutique digital architecture studio that designs premium digital experiences for businesses that charge premium prices.

Everything must communicate:

\- Precision  
\- Confidence  
\- Simplicity  
\- Timelessness  
\- Engineering Quality  
\- Luxury without showing luxury

Never loud.  
Never playful.  
Never trendy.

The experience should feel like:

\- Apple  
\- Linear  
\- Stripe  
\- Notion  
\- Clay  
\- Instrument  
\- Locomotive  
\- Framer

\# 25\. AI Design Prompt

Whenever generating any design, UI, landing page, dashboard, case study, illustration, hero image, marketing asset, or component for RED SEA DIGITAL, strictly follow this design language:

\- Quiet Luxury aesthetic.  
\- Architectural minimalism.  
\- Editorial typography with serif headlines and sans-serif UI.  
\- 12-column responsive grid.  
\- Deep Obsidian (\#111111), Alabaster (\#FBFBF9), Stone (\#F4F4F0), Champagne Gold (\#C5A059, max 2% usage).  
\- Generous whitespace (120px sections).  
\- Hairline borders (\#E5E5E0).  
\- Rounded corners (16px).  
\- Pill buttons (9999px).  
\- Lucide-style 1.5px outline icons.  
\- Soft editorial lighting and architectural photography.  
\- Premium, timeless, Awwwards-quality execution.  
\- No gradients, no glassmorphism, no neon, no visual clutter, no stock clichés.  
\- Every interface should feel engineered rather than decorated.

\---

\# Final Rule

If any design decision is uncertain:

Remove elements instead of adding them.

Increase whitespace instead of decoration.

Choose typography instead of graphics.

Prioritize clarity over novelty.

Every pixel must justify its existence.
